FAQ

Common Questions

What types of projects do you manage?
We manage residential, commercial, and hospitality projects — from single-family villas to multi-story headquarters and boutique hotels, typically ranging from $2M to $50M in construction value.
Do you work along existing architect and designer?
Absolutely. We frequently operate as the dedicated PM alongside a client's own architect, designer, or consultant team — providing the delivery structure and oversight without interfering with the creative process.
How is your PM fees structured?
We typically charge a percentage of the construction cost (usually 3–6% depending on scope and complexity), or a fixed monthly retainer for larger programmes. We provide a clear fee proposal after an initial scoping call.
At what stage should we engage?
Ideally at the very start — early PM engagement produces the best outcomes. However, we regularly step into projects mid-stream to resolve issues and restore control. It's never too late to bring in professional management.
How do you handle cost overrun or contractor issues?
Prevention is always the priority — our risk registers and change control protocols catch most issues before they become overruns. When disputes arise, we manage them directly on your behalf, supported by contract documentation we maintain from day one.
Do you manage intentional projects?
Yes. We have delivered projects across North America, Europe, and the Middle East. Our processes and reporting systems are location-agnostic, and we have established networks of local consultants and contractors in key markets.
